# Selected-run evidence inspector

The local workbench now keeps the selected run’s frozen expectation and mocked HTTP boundary visible, joins reduction candidates with their actual linked observations and assertion counts, and preserves keyboard focus when switching history. Task links organize Establish failure, Reduce steps and Check fix without removing detailed evidence sections. Dependencies use the selected job’s saved manifest; the mutable current capsule is labeled separately for new operations.

Read-only inspection rejects missing, malformed, contradictory or unsafe evidence as INCONCLUSIVE. Recorded acceptance remains historical metadata when linked evidence is unavailable. Baseline checks are distinguished from removals; rejected trials remain inspectable. A one-minimality declaration is labeled as a recorded reducer claim, not an independent verification by this view. Lifecycle completion is neutral. Malformed or absent supporting reduction evidence cannot establish minimality. This validates selected evidence association in the trusted workspace, not tamper-proof authenticity or every ancestor artifact.
