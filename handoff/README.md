# Archived engineering handoff material

These files preserve work that previously lived only in ignored local evidence directories. They are reference material, not installed product code or new verification results. The product remains in `src/`, `web/`, `tests/`, `scripts/` and the tracked fixtures. No product or diagnostic test was rerun for this handoff.

`offline-research/` contains byte-identical copies of three complete small sealed research trees:

- `contrast-transport-investigation-v1`: first uninstalled transport candidate and prospective lifecycle design, including tests and adverse evidence.
- `contrast-incremental-receiver-v1`: second uninstalled candidate, frozen 107/108 outcome, separately adjusted 108/108 result, 12-worker comparison and every adverse timing/RSS result.
- `contrast-allocation-attribution-review-v1`: focused review selecting no correction, and the unapproved single-pilot attribution plan.

`diagnostics/` contains selected authored harnesses, validators, tests, plans and reports from earlier tasks. These are partial archival copies: a historical preparation/readiness pass does not supersede the later pilot STOP. The original task directories and omitted raw evidence remain local as listed in `docs/handoff/EXCLUDED-LOCAL.md`. Copy inventories bind every included file to its original relative path and SHA256. Original files, failed attempts and frozen receipts were not rewritten.

The research trees intentionally retain original command records, workspace paths, source bindings and manifest bytes. Moving a tree does not make its recorded absolute paths valid. Several fixtures and oracles depend on omitted archived measurement rows, staged/installed executable forms, or an exact pinned Playwright bundle. Importing or running these scripts directly from a clone may fail, create outputs under their historical layout, or target local paths; do not treat them as supported package commands. Establish a new isolated, explicitly scoped execution plan before adapting them. Do not rerun sealing or benchmark scripts in the archived trees.

No candidate is installed, no lifecycle hold is approved by this copy, and no raw browser trace, screenshot batch, installed dependency tree, container image/archive, cache or secret is required to read this material. Original historical paths/hashes are provenance, not permission to access another machine. The next owner decides further work under their own instructions.
